Research

Master of Engineering thesis at HIT Lab NZ, University of Canterbury, supervised by Dr. Susanne Schmidt and Prof. Robert W. Lindeman.

“One Soul, Many Forms: The Impact of Identity Continuity on Rapport, Emotional Connectedness, and Credibility in Virtual Agents.”

I built three VR games, each with an AI agent you can talk to (a local LLM with speech in and out), and ran a 26-person controlled study. The agent that kept one identity across all three games built a stronger relationship with players than agents who met them as strangers each time.

The three games are on the work page with gameplay video.
