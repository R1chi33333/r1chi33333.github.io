Research — AI Agents × HCI

Do AI agents stay
themselves — and does
it earn our trust?

Master of Engineering thesis at the HIT Lab NZ, University of Canterbury — supervised by Dr. Susanne Schmidt & Prof. Robert W. Lindeman. The study is about interactive AI agents; VR is simply the controlled environment it runs in.

“One Soul, Many Forms: The Impact of Identity Continuity on Rapport, Emotional Connectedness, and Credibility in Virtual Agents.”

A between-subjects study testing whether an AI agent that keeps a continuous identity — while its embodiment changes across three interaction contexts — builds stronger human relationships than agents that appear as strangers each time.

Method: ANCOVA · baseline-controlled · validated rapport / connectedness / trust scales
