Research

Master of Engineering thesis at HIT Lab NZ, University of Canterbury, supervised by Dr. Susanne Schmidt and Prof. Robert W. Lindeman.

“One Soul, Many Forms: The Impact of Identity Continuity on Rapport, Emotional Connectedness, and Credibility in Virtual Agents.”

A between-subjects study testing whether an AI agent that keeps one continuous identity, while its body changes across three VR games, builds a stronger relationship with people than agents who meet them as strangers each time.

Method: ANCOVA, baseline-controlled, validated rapport, connectedness and trust scales. The three games are on the work page with gameplay video.
